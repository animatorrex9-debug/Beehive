2. TESTING STANDARD USER WORKFLOWS (Log in as user.test@example.com)
================================================================================

2.1 PROFILE COMPLETION & CURRENCY SYSTEM
----------------------------------------
- Goal: Verify that the dashboard displays balances and transactions in the user's localized currency with live conversion.
- Steps:
  1. Open Dashboard (`/dashboard`).
  2. Check the Currency selector in the top-right navbar. Switch between currencies (e.g., USD $, EUR €, GBP £, NGN ₦, BTC ₿).
  3. Verify that total balances, quick stats, and cards automatically recalculate in real-time.

2.2 KYC VERIFICATION SUBMISSION
-------------------------------
- Goal: Submit identity proof for verification.
- Steps:
  1. In the sidebar, click "KYC Verification" (`/dashboard/kyc`).
  2. Fill in:
     - Document Type (Passport / Driver's License / National ID / SSN).
     - Document ID Number.
     - Upload front & back photo of ID / proof of address.
  3. Click "Submit KYC for Review".
  4. Notice the badge updates to "Pending Verification".

2.3 DEPOSIT FLOW (CRYPTO & BANK PROOF)
--------------------------------------
- Goal: Submit a deposit request to add funds.
- Steps:
  1. Click "Deposit" (`/dashboard/deposit`).
  2. Choose deposit method:
     - Crypto (BTC / USDT / ETH): Scan QR code or copy designated wallet address.
     - Bank Transfer: View platform bank deposit details.
  3. Enter deposit amount (e.g., 500 USD or local equivalent).
  4. Upload proof of payment (screenshot/receipt).
  5. Click "Submit Deposit Request".
  6. Verify the deposit request appears in "Deposit History" with status "Pending".

2.4 MONEY TRANSFER & SEND
-------------------------
- Goal: Test peer-to-peer and external transfers.
- Steps:
  1. Click "Send Money" (`/dashboard/send`).
  2. Test all 4 transfer types:
     a. "Beehive User" -> Enter recipient email or account ID.
     b. "Local Bank Transfer" -> Enter Bank Name, Account Number, Account Name.
     c. "International Transfer" -> Enter Country, IBAN, Swift/BIC Code.
     d. "Third-Party / FinTech" -> Select PayPal / CashApp / Zelle / Venmo and handle.
  3. Enter amount (e.g., 50 local currency).
  4. Review summary with conversion and click "Send Funds".
  5. Check confirmation prompt and verify deduction in transaction history.

2.5 CURRENCY SWAP & MULTI-CURRENCY CONVERSION
---------------------------------------------
- Goal: Swap funds between fiat currencies and crypto assets.
- Steps:
  1. Click "Currency Swap" (`/dashboard/swap`).
  2. Select "From" currency (e.g., USD or NGN) and "To" currency (e.g., EUR, BTC, or USDT).
  3. Enter amount to swap.
  4. Verify live exchange rates and computed receive amount.
  5. Click "Swap Now" and verify immediate balance reallocation.

2.6 INVESTMENT PORTFOLIOS & RETURNS
-----------------------------------
- Goal: Allocate funds into automated investment tiers.
- Steps:
  1. Click "Invest" (`/dashboard/invest`).
  2. Browse available investment packages:
     - Real Estate Growth Fund
     - Crypto Yield Aggregator
     - Global Tech Index
     - Green Energy Bonds
  3. Click "Invest Now" on a selected tier.
  4. Enter amount in your local currency.
  5. Click "Confirm Investment".
  6. Verify your investment appears under "Active Investments" with bi-weekly ROI schedule.

2.7 HIGH-YIELD LOCKED SAVINGS
-----------------------------
- Goal: Move wallet balance into locked interest-bearing savings.
- Steps:
  1. Click "Accounts & Savings" (`/dashboard/accounts`).
  2. In the "Locked Savings" section, click "Move Funds to Savings".
  3. Select duration (30 Days @ 8% APY, 90 Days @ 12% APY, 1 Year @ 18% APY).
  4. Enter local currency amount (or click "MAX").
  5. Check estimated daily interest and maturity return preview.
  6. Click "Lock Funds".
  7. Verify wallet balance is deducted and savings balance increases.

2.8 VIRTUAL DEBIT & CREDIT CARDS
--------------------------------
- Goal: Request and manage virtual cards.
- Steps:
  1. Click "Cards" (`/dashboard/cards`).
  2. View your Virtual Visa / Mastercard.
  3. Toggle "Show Card Details" (Card Number, CVV, Expiry Date).
  4. Test "Freeze / Unfreeze Card" action.
  5. Test "Set Spending Limit".
  6. If card is inactive, test clicking "Activate Card" (or check status).

2.9 CHARITY & SOCIAL CAUSES DONATION
------------------------------------
- Goal: Test charity donations in local currency with accurate USD back-end processing.
- Steps:
  1. Click "Charity & Causes" (`/dashboard/charity`).
  2. Explore active campaigns (e.g., Clean Water Initiative, Disaster Relief, Youth Tech Education).
  3. Click "Donate" on a campaign.
  4. Choose a quick preset (e.g., ₦10,000 / $50) or type custom local amount.
  5. Check "Donate Anonymously" or add a donor note.
  6. Click "Confirm Donation".
  7. Verify your wallet reflects deduction, donation counter updates, and receipt is logged in `/dashboard/history`.

2.10 LOAN APPLICATION & STATUS
------------------------------
- Goal: Apply for short-term or personal loans.
- Steps:
  1. Click "Loan Application" (`/dashboard/loan-application`).
  2. Use the interactive sliders to configure:
     - Loan Amount (e.g., $5,000 / ₦500,000).
     - Repayment Duration (3 months, 6 months, 12 months).
  3. Enter Purpose of Loan and Employment details.
  4. Click "Submit Loan Application".
  5. Navigate to `/dashboard/loan-status` to track application status ("Under Review").

2.11 TAX REFUNDS & BUSINESS GRANTS
----------------------------------
- Goal: Submit tax refund claim and business grant proposals.
- Steps:
  1. Click "Tax Returns" (`/dashboard/tax-refunds`) -> Fill tax year, expected refund amount, attach W2/tax form -> Submit.
  2. Click "Grants" (`/dashboard/grants`) -> Select Grant Program (SME Booster, Tech Innovation, Green Initiative) -> Fill proposal & requested amount -> Submit.

2.12 DIRECT MESSAGING & SUPPORT CHAT
------------------------------------
- Goal: Chat in real-time with your assigned account manager.
- Steps:
  1. Click "Support & Manager Chat" (`/dashboard/chat`).
  2. View assigned manager badge (e.g., "Manager Sarah").
  3. Type and send a message (e.g., "Hello, I just submitted my KYC and deposit!").
  4. Test sending emoji or attaching document/image.
  5. Keep this tab open to test receiving replies from the manager.

2.13 ACCOUNT SETTINGS & SECURITY
--------------------------------
- Goal: Customize account preferences.
- Steps:
  1. Click "Settings" (`/dashboard/settings`).
  2. Toggle Dark Mode / Light Mode theme.
  3. Update profile bio, phone number, and notification preferences.
  4. Test Password Change form.
